====================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT PURPOSE
====================================================

Samadhan Setu is a digital platform that connects:

Citizens / Community Organizations / Panchayati Raj Institutions / ULBs / Government Departments

with:

Universities / HEIs / Students / Faculty

and:

Industries / Startups / MSMEs / CSR organizations / Research Institutions / Innovation Hubs.

The platform allows societal problems to be submitted, categorized, evaluated, assigned to suitable institutions, converted into projects, supported by industry and tracked until implementation.

Main ecosystem:

COMMUNITY PROBLEM
        ↓
SUBMISSION
        ↓
AI CATEGORIZATION
        ↓
VALIDATION / PRIORITIZATION
        ↓
UNIVERSITY MATCHING
        ↓
STUDENT + FACULTY TEAM
        ↓
INDUSTRY COLLABORATION
        ↓
PROJECT / PROTOTYPE
        ↓
PILOT / IMPLEMENTATION
        ↓
MEASURABLE COMMUNITY IMPACT

This is a FRONTEND PROTOTYPE.
Use realistic mock data.
No real Aadhaar verification, payment processing, government APIs or external authentication are required.
